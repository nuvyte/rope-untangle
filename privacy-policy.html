Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-03

Rope Untangle ("the app") is developed by Nuvyte. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

Information we collect

None. The app does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal or usage information. There are no accounts, no ads, and no analytics.

On launch, the app downloads a small version file to check whether an update is available. No personal information is included in or derived from this request.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (unlocked levels, best scores, daily streak, theme and sound settings) is saved only on your device. It never leaves your device, and it is deleted if you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Permissions

The app uses vibration for haptic feedback. It does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, or files.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new date.
